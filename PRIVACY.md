# Finara Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Finara is a personal finance app for tracking income, expenses, budgets, bills and saving goals. This policy explains what data Finara uses and how.

## Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in Finara is stored **only on your device**: transactions, budgets, saving goals, upcoming bills, notes and app settings. This data is not sent to our servers or to anyone else, and we can't see it.

## Account data

To sign in, Finara uses **Firebase Authentication** by Google. When you create an account or sign in, Firebase processes:

- your email address;
- your name, if you enter it or sign in with Google;
- your password, if you sign up with email (Firebase stores it in a protected form; we never see it);
- technical data that Firebase needs to keep your sign-in secure, such as the device's IP address.

This data is used only to let you sign in. Google's processing is described in the [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
- We don't upload your financial data anywhere.

## Exporting your data

You can export your transactions to a CSV file at any time: **Profile → Export to CSV**. The file is shared only where you choose to send it.

## Deleting your data

- **Reset app data** (Profile → Reset app data) deletes all financial data on the device and keeps your account.
- **Delete account** (Profile → Delete account) permanently deletes your Firebase account and all Finara data on the device.
- Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored on the device.

## Children

Finara is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.
